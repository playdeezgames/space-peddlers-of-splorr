## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/space-peddlers-of-splorr/

**Space Peddlers of SPLORR!!** is a faithful port of **Space Trader 1.2.2**, Pieter Spronck's trading game for Palm OS, to the browser.

Start with a Gnat, a thousand credits and sixteen skill points. Buy cargo where it is cheap, warp to where it is dear, and sell. Upgrade yer ship, hire mercenaries, and decide what to do about the pirates, the police and the traders you meet on the way: fight, flee, surrender, bribe, or plunder. Retire rich, or buy a moon.

- The original's rules, numbers and text: 120 solar systems, ten trade goods, special events and quests, news, loans and insurance, high scores.
- The game is the original C, translated mechanically into Odin; the Palm OS it ran on is rebuilt underneath it, forms, alerts, menus and all.
- It saves itself in yer browser, and picks up where you left off.

### How to play

Tap with the mouse or a finger, as on the Palm. Letters are the original's shortcuts (B buy, S sell, Y shipyard, W warp, G galaxy, I info, H help); Esc or F2 opens the menu, Enter confirms, the arrows page. On a phone, the Menu, arrow and Keyboard buttons under the screen do the same.

### About it

The pictures are placeholders: the original art is Alexander Lawrence's and is not covered by the GPL, so it is not used. New art is coming.

This is not an official Space Trader release; Pieter Spronck asked that new versions not use that name.
