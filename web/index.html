Tap with the mouse or a finger. Keys: letters are the original's shortcuts (B buy, S sell, Y shipyard, W warp, G galaxy, I info, H help), Esc or F2 opens the menu, Enter confirms, arrows page.

An Odin/WebAssembly port of Space Trader 1.2.2 by Pieter Spronck (GNU GPL; additional coding by Sam Anderson and Samuel Goldstein). Source: github.com/playdeezgames/space-peddlers-of-splorr (GPL). Font m6x11 by Daniel Linssen. Ported with an AI agent (Claude Code); the original pictures are not used.
