Space Peddlers of SPLORR!! has been playable on GitHub Pages since November 20. Today it gets a page here too.

It is Space Trader 1.2.2, Pieter Spronck's trading game for Palm OS, in the browser. Buy cargo where it is cheap, warp to where it is dear, sell, and deal with the pirates, police and traders on the way.

- **Faithful:** the game is the original C, translated mechanically into Odin, with the original's rules, numbers and text.
- **Palm OS rebuilt underneath:** forms, alerts, help, menus and saves all work the way they did on the Palm.
- **Tap or type:** the original's letter shortcuts work, and the buttons under the screen bring up the menu and a phone's keyboard.
- **Free software:** GPL, like the original. The source is at https://github.com/playdeezgames/space-peddlers-of-splorr

The pictures are placeholders; the original art is not covered by the GPL.

The latest version always lives at https://playdeezgames.github.io/space-peddlers-of-splorr/ and the itch build can lag behind it.
